## Architectural constraints from the comparison

V1 employee bank numbers, bearer tokens, role shortcuts, night shift labels, inventory issuance actions and employee mock records must not be ported into V2. V2 retains company-scoped permissions, effective navigation, explicit employee links, separately owned Accounting and Pakistan FBR invoices, and backend-authoritative financial/HR state.
